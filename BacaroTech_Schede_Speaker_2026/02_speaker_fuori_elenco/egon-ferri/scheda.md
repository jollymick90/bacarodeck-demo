## Biografia pubblica

Putting AI into production is not just about connecting a model to a chat interface. In Gamindo AI, we built a workflow that turns vague requirements, source materials and live game state into structured game-based learning content: it refines the initial intent, generates a validated learning experience, supports controlled edits through structured patches, and produces contextual commentary and audio aligned with gameplay.

This talk walks through the architecture behind it: typed contracts with PydanticAI, FastAPI endpoints, fallback strategies across OpenAI, Anthropic and Google, streaming events, output validation, eval datasets, custom evaluators, observability with Logfire, and practical error handling.

Attendees will leave with patterns and tradeoffs for designing AI workflows that are robust, observable, measurable and flexible enough to evolve without rewriting everything.
