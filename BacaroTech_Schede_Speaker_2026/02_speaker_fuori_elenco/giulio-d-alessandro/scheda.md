## Biografia pubblica

Giulio D'Alessandro is a philosophy graduate who turned to software. He joined Poste Italiane five years ago and started out as a DevOps engineer.
Then AI arrived and sent him back where he began. When machines can build anything, the rare skill is knowing what's worth building — and why.
He now works in Poste Italiane's Digital Technology Office, between the rigour of formal models and the messiness of what people actually mean.
