## Biografia pubblica

Alice Viola Setti is a proud neurodivergent transgender woman, software engineer, architect, and sysadmin with a master's degree in Mechatronic Engineering and a product-builder mindset.

Her background spans the entire technical spectrum, from provisioning bare-metal datacenters to architecting complex distributed systems and leading engineering teams. She is highly autonomous and drives end-to-end product delivery.

She works mainly with Kubernetes, Kafka, Postgres, C++, Node, and Vue, and has shipped with Python, Ruby, Objective-C++, Swift, Rust, OpenCV, Proxmox, VMware, Nginx, HAProxy, Scylla, Redis, MySQL, MariaDB, MongoDB, OpenSearch, Solr, Neo4j, Cassandra, Pulsar, RabbitMQ, NATS, and MQTT.

She has installed and managed clusters in on-premise datacenters using bare-metal servers, hypervisors, and Kubernetes; configured network switches and firewalls; developed microservice architectures; built drone control systems; and conducted research in computer vision.
