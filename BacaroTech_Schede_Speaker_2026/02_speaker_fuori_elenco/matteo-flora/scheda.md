## Biografia pubblica

Matteo Flora is Professor of Fundamentals of AI and SuperIntelligence Safety at the European School of Economics and contract Professor of Corporate Reputation and Crisis Management at Università di Pavia. He combines cognitive science, data and generative AI to shape how organisations, ideas and audiences build knowledge and make choices.

A serial entrepreneur, he founded The Fool through Samadhi Holding, co-founded The Magician, 40Degrees and LT42, and is Founding Partner of 42 Law Firm.

A cybersecurity expert and 2012 US Department of State IVLP participant, he is President of PermessoNegato APS, supporting victims of non-consensual pornography, and co-founder of the Hermes Centre for Digital Human Rights.

He hosts “Intelligenze Artifici” on Mediaset/TgCom and the tech policy YouTube channel “Ciao Internet”. A lecturer and keynote speaker, he is a three-time TEDx speaker and former Forbes podcaster.
