## Biografia pubblica

Alessandro Riboni is a Staff Engineer at Generali, specializing in AI engineering and GenAI platforms. Supports internal teams and builds horizontal tooling that standardizes and simplifies GenAI development: from a framework for prompt management, automated evaluation and CI/CD-based release of prompts, to Flowmetrics, a semantic layer that gives AI agents governed access to business metrics. Before that, five years in consulting for insurance and banking: first as a data engineer on real-time pipelines, then as an ML engineer building LLM-based claims assistants, RAG systems for policy risk assessment and document-parsing tools on AWS. Holds an MSc in Data Science from the University of Milano-Bicocca.
