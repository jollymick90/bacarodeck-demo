## Biografia pubblica

Saverio Murgia is CTO Italy at Wonderful, an enterprise agentic AI platform that enables large organizations to build their own Enterprise Super Intelligence through conversational and back-office agents. With almost ten years of experience taking AI products from zero to real-world impact, he co-founded Horus Technology in 2014, an AI company dedicated to improving the lives of blind and visually impaired people, leading it to a successful exit in 2019. He later led product development at Caricami and spent over two years as CTO of a stealth startup studio in Los Angeles, contributing to the launch of more than 20 ventures across SaaS, AI and B2B/B2C products. He holds an MSc in Advanced Robotics and a BSc in Biomedical Engineering, with research experience at EPFL, IIT and Ecole Centrale de Nantes, and is an alumnus of ISICT, the school of excellence of the University of Genoa.
