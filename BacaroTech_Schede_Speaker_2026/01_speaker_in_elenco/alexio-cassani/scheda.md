## Biografia pubblica

Alexio Cassani is an Italian entrepreneur and thought leader in generative AI, AI agents, and corporate digital transformation. He is co-founder of FairMind, a startup that is building AI agent solutions that boost software development team productivity.
A Computer Engineering graduate from Politecnico di Milano (2003), he was CTO at Cortilia and Webscience (now part of the Adesso group), founded the retail and e-commerce startup Stentle (acquired in 2019), and worked as a researcher with the Intranet and Cloud Observatories at Politecnico di Milano.
He is adjunct professor at Politecnico di Milano School of Management, teaching Generative AI and advising companies on AI adoption and transformation. A frequent speaker at Italian and international conferences on generative AI, AI agents, software factory innovation, and responsible technology adoption, he is the author of "Code Revealed", a guide to how roles, organization, and tools in software development are evolving in the AI era.
