## Biografia pubblica

Giorgio Natili is a technologist, ethicist, and builder working at the intersection of artificial intelligence, human dignity, and data sovereignty. As Senior Director and Head of AI Engineering for Oracle Cloud Support, he leads the design and deployment of AI systems that make enterprise support smarter, faster, and more trustworthy at scale.

His expertise in Sovereign AI—the principle that individuals, organizations, and nations must retain control over their data, models, and AI-driven decisions—comes from years of hands-on work. At OPAQUE Systems, where he served as VP and Head of Engineering, he built confidential AI infrastructure that enabled insights from sensitive data without exposing it. This shaped his belief that ethical AI and sovereign AI are inseparable.

With experience at Amazon, Mozilla, Capital One, OPAQUE Systems, and Oracle, he focuses on building AI that is powerful without being exploitative.
