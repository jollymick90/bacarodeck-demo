## Biografia pubblica

Alfonso Fuggetta is a Full Professor of Computer Science at Politecnico di Milano, where his work spans software engineering and digital transformation in their technical and managerial dimensions. He is the former CEO and Scientific Director of Cefriel and former Chief Digital Transformation Officer of Ferrari, and has been a Visiting Professor at NTNU, CU Boulder, and UC Irvine. For over thirty years, he has worked at the crossroads of software engineering research, industry, and innovation, advising companies and public institutions on digital transformation and the impact of AI. He is ranked among the top 2% of researchers worldwide (Elsevier/Stanford) and writes about technology and society on his Substack, "A bassa voce".
